  Japanese Practice — built into our workflow

  Each interaction, I'll include a 練習 (れんしゅう, renshuu) — practice block when relevant:

  練習 renshuu
  Try typing these:
  - かぎ → 鍵 (key) — type kagi in hiragana mode, then press Space to convert
  - もり → 守 (guardian) — type mori, Space to convert
  - ちょう → 帳 (ledger) — type chou, Space to convert

  On your Japanese keyboard: the key labeled 半角/全角 (top-left, near Esc) or かな (right of Space) toggles input mode. In
  hiragana mode, type romaji and it converts to hiragana automatically. Press Space to see kanji candidates, Enter to confirm.
