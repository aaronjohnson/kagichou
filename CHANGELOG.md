# 鍵帳 kagichou — Chronicle

*A ledger kept in the manner of fortress records, marking the deeds of keys and the words of those who came before.*

---

## 8th of Granite, Year One (2026-03-06)

*"Even a good person attains birth in the Pure Land, so it goes without saying that an evil person will."*
— Shinran (親鸞), Tannishō (歎異抄), Chapter 3

The keeper arrived at the village with four Titan keys in hand.
Kambei (温) surveyed the perimeter from the hilltop, counting entrances.
Kyūzō (故) said nothing and polished his blade.
Heihachi (知) laughed, split a log, and asked what was for dinner.
Kikuchiyo (菊千代/新) waved a stolen ancestral scroll and demanded to be registered first.

A ledger was opened. Inventories were taken in TOML.
No codes were spent. No bandits arrived.

> *Four keys present. Zero registrations. The village sleeps unguarded.*

---

## 8th of Granite, Year One — Evening (2026-03-08)

*"The purpose of the appearing of Amida in this world lay in His revealing the truth of the Original Vow alone."*
— Shinran (親鸞), Kyōgyōshinshō (教行信証)

The keeper studied the old ways to understand the new (温故知新).
Two names were chosen for the work ahead:

- **鍵守 kagimori** — the guardian, who watches over the keys
- **鍵帳 kagichou** — the ledger, where all is recorded

The ledger was given its proper name.
A .gitignore was placed at the gate: no secrets shall pass.

> *The village now has a name. The guardian will arrive when the forge is ready.*
